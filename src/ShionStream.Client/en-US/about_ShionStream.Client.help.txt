    - API version: 1.0.0
    - SDK version: 0.1.2
    - Generator version: 7.26.0
    - Build package: org.openapitools.codegen.languages.PowerShellClientCodegen

    Frameworks supported:

    * PowerShell 6.2 or later
    * .NET 4.0 or later
